# Project Codename: The Iceberg of Magic

## Overview

A fantasy RPG progression system centered around the discovery of increasingly rare and mysterious forms of magic. The world believes it understands magic, but the truth is hidden beneath centuries of lost history, forbidden knowledge, and ancient secrets.

The entire magic ecosystem is represented as an iceberg.

The visible portion contains the magic known to civilization.

The hidden portion contains increasingly dangerous and mysterious powers that most people do not even know exist.

At the very bottom lies the ultimate secret:

**Authority.**

---

# Core Philosophy

Magic is not simply a skill.

Magic is knowledge.

Every higher tier represents a deeper understanding of reality itself.

Most people spend their entire lives mastering only the visible layers.

Only a handful ever discover what lies beneath.

---

# The Iceberg Structure

## Visible Layers

### Tier 1 — Arcane Magic

The foundation of all magical study.

This is not a collection of predefined spells.

Instead, Arcane Magic functions as a magical language.

Players create their own spells through magical diagrams, glyphs, circles, and symbols.

### Characteristics

* Most common form of magic
* Taught in academies
* Accessible to nearly everyone
* Requires creativity and experimentation
* Foundation for all higher magic

### Inspiration

* Witch Hat Atelier
* Magical engineering
* Rune crafting
* Symbolic language systems

---

## Tier 2 — Elemental Magic

The first specialization layer.

Instead of creating generic magical effects, users begin channeling natural forces.

### Possible Branches

* Fire
* Water
* Ice
* Earth
* Metal
* Wind
* Lightning
* Nature

### Notes

Different races may possess natural affinities.

A race with strong fire affinity may learn fire techniques more efficiently.

---

## Tier 3 — Advanced Magic

Magic begins manipulating concepts rather than simple elements.

Examples:

* Gravity
* Light
* Shadow
* Sound
* Crystal
* Blood
* Spirit

These disciplines are considered rare and prestigious.

---

## Tier 4 — High Magic

The highest publicly known level.

Only legendary archmages typically reach this stage.

### Examples

* Space manipulation
* Time manipulation
* Soul manipulation
* Reality distortion

Most people believe this is the pinnacle of magic.

They are wrong.

---

# Hidden Layers

Everything below this point is largely unknown.

Most books never mention these powers.

Entire kingdoms may not know they exist.

---

## Tier 5 — Cursed Magic

The first hidden layer.

Known only through myths and warnings.

### Characteristics

* Magic obtained through suffering
* Permanent drawbacks
* Physical corruption
* Mental deterioration

Examples:

* Life-draining spells
* Pain conversion
* Curse implantation

---

## Tier 6 — Dark Magic

The deliberate embrace of forbidden power.

Unlike Curses, Dark Magic is pursued willingly.

### Characteristics

* Forbidden knowledge
* Rituals
* Sacrifices
* Hidden organizations

Examples:

* Necromancy
* Shadow possession
* Demon summoning
* Forbidden transformations

---

## Tier 7 — Chaos Magic

Magic that does not obey conventional rules.

Most scholars consider it impossible.

### Characteristics

* Unpredictable
* Wild
* Reality-breaking

Examples:

* Randomized effects
* Probability manipulation
* Paradox creation
* Reality instability

---

## Tier 8 — Primordial Magic

Ancient power from before civilization.

The original forces that shaped existence.

### Characteristics

* Extremely rare
* Ancient origins
* Tied to creation myths

Examples

* World-shaping
* Planetary influence
* Creation and destruction

---

## Tier 9 — Divine Magic

Power connected to gods, divine beings, or cosmic entities.

### Characteristics

* Faith-based
* Celestial origin
* Miraculous effects

Examples

* Blessings
* Miracles
* Resurrection
* Divine judgment

---

## Tier 10 — Authority

The deepest layer.

The true bottom of the iceberg.

The secret hidden from the world.

---

# Authority System

Authority is not magic.

Authority is control.

Instead of casting spells, Authority grants dominion over a concept.

Examples:

### Authority of Death

Control over death itself.

Not death magic.

Death.

### Authority of Space

Control over spatial existence.

Not teleportation.

Space itself.

### Authority of Time

Control over temporal flow.

Not time spells.

Time itself.

### Authority of Shadows

Control over all shadows.

Anywhere.

At any time.

---

## Rules of Authority

### Rule 1

Authorities cannot be learned through normal study.

### Rule 2

Authorities cannot be crafted through spell circles.

### Rule 3

Authorities choose their wielder.

### Rule 4

Most Authorities can only have one active holder.

### Rule 5

Authorities exist outside the normal magic hierarchy.

Not restrictions.

Affinities.

---

Exceptional Arcane and Elemental growth.

---

Exceptional magical engineering.

## Celestial

Divine Magic affinity.

Rare race.

# Arcane Magic Creation System

## Design Goal

Allow players to create unique spells.

No predefined spell list.

Players become spell designers.

---

# Spell Circle System

Players create magical diagrams.

A spell is constructed from multiple components.

---

## Core Structure

### Central Rune

Defines primary function.

Examples:

* Fire
* Water
* Force
* Light

---

### Shape Layer

Determines behavior.

Examples:

Triangle

* Projectile

Square

* Barrier

Circle

* Area Effect

Star

* Complex Function

---

### Modifier Runes

Additional effects.

Examples:

* Increase range
* Increase duration
* Split projectiles
* Increase penetration
* Tracking

---

### Stability Score

Every spell receives a stability rating.

Factors:

* Complexity
* Rune compatibility
* Mana efficiency

Low stability can result in:

* Spell failure
* Misfires
* Explosions
* Corruption

---

# Spell Discovery

Players begin with basic runes.

Additional runes are obtained through:

* Dungeon exploration
* Ancient ruins
* Boss rewards
* Research
* Lost civilizations

---

# Otherworld Dungeons

## Concept

Portals occasionally appear across the world.

These portals lead to separate dimensions.

Each dimension contains:

* Monsters
* Resources
* Lost knowledge
* Ancient runes
* Hidden magic tiers

---

## Importance

Dungeons are the primary source of progression.

The deepest secrets of the iceberg are hidden within these worlds.

---

# Main Narrative Mystery

The world believes High Magic is the peak.

However:

* Curses exist.
* Dark Magic exists.
* Chaos exists.
* Primordial power exists.
* Divine truths exist.

And beneath everything...

Authority waits.

The central question of the story becomes:

> "Who was the first Authority Holder, and why was the existence of Authority erased from history?"

---

# Endgame Vision

Players begin as students learning to draw simple magical circles.

They end by uncovering truths powerful enough to reshape reality itself.

The journey is not from weak to strong.

The journey is from ignorant to enlightened.
